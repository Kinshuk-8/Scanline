How it works

~3–10 seconds per document
01

Upload or drop a file

A text-based PDF or a photo of a printed page — a scan, a screenshot, even a picture taken on your phone.

02

Text gets extracted

PDFs are parsed directly; images run through on-device OCR. Either way, extraction happens in your browser tab.

03

Read the summary

Pick short, medium, or long, skim the key points, and copy or download whatever you need.
